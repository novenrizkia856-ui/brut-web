# Product Positioning

## Primary Positioning

**A trustless marketplace for renting GPU compute.**

## Short Description

BRUT connects idle GPU supply with people who need compute and coordinates payment through onchain escrow.

## One-Line Message

**Idle GPUs. Real jobs. Paid onchain.**

## Product Value

For buyers:

- discover independent GPU supply,
- compare price and provider history,
- lock payment under known rules,
- and view verification evidence before settlement.

For providers:

- monetize idle GPU capacity,
- prove that a buyer has funded payment,
- build portable reputation,
- and receive automatic settlement after valid completion.

## Core Differentiator

The product is not only a directory of GPU providers.

Its central feature is the combination of:

- matching,
- escrow,
- verification,
- status,
- reputation,
- and onchain settlement.
