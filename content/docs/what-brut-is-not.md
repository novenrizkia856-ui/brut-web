# What BRUT Is Not

BRUT has a narrow role.

It is not:

- a centralized cloud provider,
- a company that owns the GPUs being rented,
- a native-token trading platform,
- a wallet,
- a general freelance marketplace,
- a hardware manufacturer,
- a GPU reseller,
- or a price speculation product.

BRUT's purpose is:

> Match idle GPU supply with compute demand and make the economic exchange enforceable through onchain rules.

The actual compute remains on provider hardware.
